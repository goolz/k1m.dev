How it works

From brief to live site — a transparent, fast process with no surprises.

01

Discovery & Design

We define your goals, target audience, and page structure. I produce a design direction that matches your brand, or we start from an existing design in Figma.

02

AI builds the site

Claude writes clean, semantic HTML/CSS/JS — section by section. You review and approve as we go. Every component is consistent, accessible, and SEO-ready.

03

Deploy & connect

The repo is pushed to GitHub, a GitHub Actions pipeline is wired up, and the site goes live on GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages in minutes.

04

Iterate forever

Need a new page, updated copy, or a redesigned section? Describe it. AI writes it. We review, commit, push. Your site evolves without a dev ticket queue.
